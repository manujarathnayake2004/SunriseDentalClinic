Using the system

Follow these steps when setting up and operating Sunrise Dental Clinic.

1

Initial setup

On a new database, create the first administrator account. No default usernames or passwords are provided.

2

Configure clinic data

An administrator adds dentists, treatment types, treatment fees, consultation fees and service durations before appointments are created.

3

Sign in

Use an authorized staff username and password. Administrators can create additional Admin or Receptionist accounts.

4

Register patient

Open Patients, select Add Patient and enter the patient name, address and valid contact number.

5

Create appointment

Select the patient, dentist, treatment, date and time. The system blocks past times, invalid 15-minute intervals, treatments that would finish after closing, and any dentist booking that overlaps another treatment duration.

6

Search appointment

Use the Appointments search box with the unique appointment number, patient name or dentist name to display appointment details.

7

Complete treatment

Open Appointment Details and mark the scheduled appointment as Completed after the treatment is finished.

8

Generate bill

Open Billing or a completed appointment. The separate billing web service calculates treatment fee plus consultation fee.

9

Print receipt and reports

Print the patient receipt, then use Reports to review appointment activity, completion rate, dentist workload and revenue. Appointment reports can also be exported as CSV.

10

Secure logout

Use Exit System / Secure Logout when finishing work. The authenticated session is invalidated, authentication is cleared and the session cookie is removed before returning to login.
